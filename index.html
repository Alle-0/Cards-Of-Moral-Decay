How to Play

01

The Black Card

Each round starts with a wild provocation. The Dominus awaits your most immoral response.

02

The Completion

Choose from your White Cards the one that maximizes the discomfort or hilarity of the jury.

03

The Judgment

The vote is anonymous. The Dominus chooses the worst combination. Winners accumulate points and DC.

04

Dirty Cash

Use your dirty earnings in the Black Market to unlock exclusive themes, skins, and frames.

Unique Mechanics

Chaos Engine

Pure Entropy

Every 2 rounds, a random event flips the rules: Inflation (double points), Dictatorship (forced hand swap), or Blackout (total censorship).

Progression

Criminal Ranks

Climb the social hierarchy of decay. From Pure Soul to the mythical title of Apocalyptic Entity. Prove you have no conscience.

Unpredictable Rando

Rand Bot

No friends at your level? Rand Bot plays with you. It has no logic, no morals, and wins more often than you'd like to admit.

Customization

Black Market

Unlock atmospheric themes (Asylum, Kyoto, Lucifer) and exclusive skins like Midas or Noir to personalize your experience.
