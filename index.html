Cards of Moral Decay is an online multiplayer card game rooted in dark humor, designed as a free, uncensored digital alternative to classic physical party games for adults. Playable instantly as a Web App directly in your browser or downloadable via an APK file for Android smartphones. It features private multiplayer lobbies, integrated AI opponents (Rand Bot), real-time synchronization, and extensive cosmetic customization. Perfect for game nights, it's the ultimate dark humor app for groups. Also indexable and available through alternative gaming stores like Itch.io and Uptodown.
